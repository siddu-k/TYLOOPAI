Problem & Motivation

Why Traditional AI Tools Fall Short for Students

Standard AI chatbots rely entirely on long walls of text. Students learn much better when they can see visual diagrams, hear explanations, and practice interactively.

1. Text Overload

Reading long paragraphs of text makes complex engineering concepts hard to visualize and mentally exhausting for students to follow.

Hard to Visualize

2. Lack of Human Touch

Plain chat boxes feel cold and distant. Having a digital teacher that speaks and gestures makes learning much more engaging and natural.

Passive Reading

3. Internet & Privacy Risks

Most online AI tools stop working without Wi-Fi and require uploading private university study documents to public servers.

Privacy Concerns

System Overview

How Tyloop AI Works in 3 Simple Steps

A straightforward learning pipeline that turns student questions into spoken lessons, visual blackboards, and interactive practice tests.

1

Student Input

Students can speak using their microphone, upload PDF study materials, or type questions directly into the chat interface.

2

Smart AI Processing Engine

Processes the student's request using either ultra-fast cloud AI or 100% private offline AI that runs right on the laptop without internet.

3

Interactive Visual & Spoken Lessons

The 3D digital teacher speaks the explanation out loud, the smart blackboard draws visual diagrams, and practice tests assess learning.

Core Feature 1

Smart Dual AI Engine: Cloud Speed + Offline Privacy

Students can seamlessly switch between high-speed cloud intelligence and 100% private offline AI depending on their needs.

Cloud Mode (Fast & Powerful)

Uses Google Gemini Cloud AI for instant answers, detailed presentation deck creation, and complex problem solving when internet is available.

• Sub-second response speed
• Ideal for generating full presentations & diagrams
• Automatic health indicator in top navigation bar

Offline Mode (100% Private)

Runs directly on the user's laptop using Ollama. Requires zero internet connection, protecting student privacy and confidential documents.

• Works anywhere without Wi-Fi connection
• Zero monthly fees or data usage
• In-app download progress indicator for AI models
